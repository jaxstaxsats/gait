Strength circuit

Built for your exact equipment. Every exercise earns its place.

Goblet Wall Sit

60 sec

Safest VMO builder — no kneecap irritation

Back against wall, thighs parallel, medicine ball at chest. Hold and breathe. Don't let your knees cave.

Single-Leg Glute Bridge

3 × 12

Targets the glute weakness causing knee collapse

Lie on back, lift one leg straight. Drive through your heel. Squeeze at the top for 1 second.

Weighted Calf Raises

3 × 15

Addresses calf strain history — eccentric resilience

Step edge, both dumbbells. Rise slowly, full heel drop on the way down. Control the negative.

Ab Roller

3 × 10

Core stability prevents pelvic drop mid-run

Neutral spine throughout. Roll out slowly, pull back from the core. Stop before form fails.

Morning routine

Every day, before anything else. 15 to 45 minutes. This is training — not optional.

Figure-4 replaces Knee-to-Chest today — groin sensitivity at 2/10. Return when 0/10.

🌅

Cat-Cow Mobility

1 min / 10 reps

On hands and knees. Arch slowly, drop slowly. Move with your breath — this is where the day starts.

Wakes up the spine after sleep — essential before any run

⚡

Glute Bridges

2 × 12 reps

Heels flat, squeeze glutes hard at the top. Hold for 1 second. Feel this in your glutes, not your back.

Activates glutes so they protect the knee on every run

🔄

90/90 Hip Switches

10 reps each side

Both knees at 90°. Rotate hips to switch — no hands. Keep your torso tall throughout.

Critical for hilly terrain — poor hip rotation feeds knee problems
